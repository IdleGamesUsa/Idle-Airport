✈️What is Idle Airport?

Idle Airport is an idle / incremental game: you run an airport that keeps earning money even when you are not playing. You start with $10,000 and one regional gate with a Cessna 208B Grand Caravan EX. Every departing plane pays you for its passengers; you reinvest that money in more gates, bigger planes, upgrades and new terminals, until you own 5 terminals, 50 gates and the biggest jets in the sky. Then you can prestige and start over in a much richer game.

🔁How you earn (the core loop)

  • Gates earn all the time. Every built gate has one plane. Each cycle passengers board, the plane departs, and after a turnaround the next plane arrives. A gate's income flows into your cash continuously, whether you watch it or not.
  • Watching pays a lump sum. On the Gate screen, the gate you are looking at banks its income and pays it as one lump sum when the plane departs. Watch a whole boarding from the start and you also get 1 free plane ticket (up to 100 per rolling 24 hours).
  • Pay per departure = every boarded passenger × the plane's passenger bonus. Business pays 2.5× and First 5× a Coach seat. Later gates in a terminal pay more: +6% per gate number (Gate 10 pays +54% vs. Gate 1).
  • Full terminals pay extra. Building all 10 gates of a terminal gives that terminal ×1.25; getting all 10 of its planes to tier 5 gives ×1.5 more.
  • Reinvest. Build gates in order (Gate 1 → 10), upgrade planes (tier 1 → 5), research global upgrades, unlock the next terminal.

Idle & offline earnings

While the app is closed your gates keep earning at 100% of your live rate (no penalty) for up to 4 hours. When you come back a Welcome back popup shows what you earned; collect it, or watch an optional ad to double it. Upgrade timers also keep running while you are away, and an upgrade that finishes during your absence raises your income from that moment on.

How full a plane is depends on Plane popularity: at Lv 0 only 10% of the seats get a passenger (empty seats stay visible in the terminal); at Lv 100 every seat is filled.

Buying a level pays cash now and starts a real-time timer; the effect applies when the timer finishes, even while the app is closed. Each line runs one level at a time. Each next level costs ×1.17 the previous one. New numbers apply from the next plane that arrives.

🚚Ground services

From Terminal 2 on, each gate can get its own ground crew. On the Gate screen, tap the green + at a vehicle's spot next to the plane to unlock that service for that gate (a one-time purchase). Once unlocked, the vehicle drives up and works while each plane boards, and the gate's departure income goes up by the service's bonus. Bonuses of different services add up.

🧳 Baggage service · T2–T5A tug brings 3 baggage carts round behind the right wing; a conveyor belt docks at the back-right door.
⛽ Fuel service · T3–T5A long fuel truck parks at the front of the right wing and runs a hose under the wing.
🍽 Catering service · T4–T5A catering truck noses up to the rear-left door, raises its box and rolls the trolleys on board.

🎫Plane tickets

Tickets are the game's premium currency, and in the test build you earn them by playing:

  • 1 free ticket for every plane you watch board and depart from the start (up to 100 per rolling 24 hours).
  • Milestones (555 tickets in total), daily rewards (days 2, 6 and 7) and event milestones.

Spend them to finish an upgrade timer instantly (4 per hour left, min. 1), or turn them into cash in the Store: 5 tickets = 15 minutes of your current income (packs of 5 / 20 / 50 / 100 tickets). If you are short of cash when buying something, the game can offer to cover the rest with tickets.

⚡Rewarded ads & boosts

Ads are always optional; you never have to watch one. In the Android app they are short rewarded video ads (Google AdMob). Watching one gives you exactly one of these, depending on the button you tapped:

ButtonReward
▶ Watch ad · +15 min 2x boost+15 minutes of 2× income. The ad button tops up to 2 hours (8 ads); it never shortens a longer boost.
▶ Watch ad to double (Welcome back)Doubles the money your gates earned while you were away.
▶ Watch ad to double (Daily reward)Doubles the income and boost parts of today's daily reward (tickets are not doubled).
▶ Ad on a running upgrade−30 minutes on that upgrade's timer (once every 30 minutes, shown only while more than 30 min remain).

Free boosts from daily rewards add their time on top of any running boost and can go past 2 hours. The header's pay bonus shows ⚡ while a boost is running.

Rare flights

Very rarely (about 1 in 1,000 watched planes), a special flight replaces the plane at the gate you are watching. When it departs you get 10 hours of income at once plus a 5× income boost for 2 hours (it stacks with the 2× boost), and the first time, the Gold Berry Crest frame.

📦Events

Events are limited-time side games. When one is running, a banner appears under the header; tap it to open the event. Event time follows the real clock, so it keeps going while the app is closed (up to your offline cap), and it never pauses or slows your airport.

★Prestige

Once you own all 5 terminals, all 50 gates and have every plane at its top tier (global upgrades are not required), you can pay the prestige price to restart in a richer game. There are 10 prestige levels; the checklist is at the bottom of the Upgrades tab.

  • Each level multiplies income, every price and your starting cash by ×8,250: Terminal 1 after prestige 1 pays what an 11th terminal would. The climb feels the same, with bigger numbers.
  • The price rises about 20% per level (on top of the multiplier).
  • Each level awards a ★ prestige frame for your pilot, equipped automatically.

💡Tips for new players

  • Watch your planes on the Gate screen when you play: you get the lump-sum payout and a free ticket per full boarding (up to 100 a day).
  • Start research early. Upgrade timers run while the app is closed, so start a level before you put the phone down. Plane popularity fills empty seats and raises the pay per passenger.
  • Check in at least every 4 hours: offline income stops counting after 4 h.
  • Finish terminals. All 10 gates built = ×1.25 for that terminal; all 10 at tier 5 = ×1.5 more.
  • Claim every day (Day 7 is a chest) and claim milestones: each one is +4% pay forever, even after prestige.
  • Ground services from Terminal 2: baggage is the cheapest service; one research level boosts that service at every gate you own it.
  • Tap any i button in the game for the exact numbers.

❓FAQ

Do I earn money while the app is closed?

Yes: 100% of your live income for up to 4 hours, and upgrade timers keep running. Collect it in the Welcome back popup (optionally double it with an ad).

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads are optional rewards (2× boost time, doubling a reward, or −30 min on an upgrade). The game is fully playable without them.

Where is my progress saved?

Locally on your device; there is no account or sign-in. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes your progress, so keep the app installed.

Can I buy anything?

Not in the test build. In-app purchases and online leaderboards are coming later. Tickets are earned by playing.

Why are my planes half empty?

The share of seats filled comes from the Plane popularity upgrade: 10% at Lv 0–1, 100% at Lv 100.

What does "Tickets 3/100" in the header mean?

The second number counts the free tickets you earned by watching departures in the last 24 hours (max 100). Your ticket balance is shown next to it.

Does upgrading a plane reset my ground services?

No. Services stay with the gate when you upgrade its plane. Only prestige resets them.
