Privacy Policy

Idle Airport · by Idle Games USA · Effective date: October 5, 2026 · Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Idle Airport (the “Game”) stores and what information third-party services used by the Game may collect. “We”, “us” and “Idle Games USA” refer to the developer of the Game. Contact: idlegamesusa@gmail.com.

Status note (October 6, 2026): the Android app is in testing on Google Play. Optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob are active in the Android app, and Google’s User Messaging Platform shows a consent form where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). Optional in-app purchases use Google Play Billing; they are switched on only when the products are available in Google Play. Online leaderboards (Google Play Games Services) are not active yet; they are described below as planned (“when enabled”). We do not operate a backend server for the Game and we do not use any analytics service of our own.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-up is needed to play.
  • Your game progress is saved locally on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The Android app shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Optional purchases use Google Play Billing. Optional leaderboards (Google Play Games Services) are planned. These services are provided by Google and are governed by Google’s own policies as well as this one.
  • Data sent over the network by the app and by Google’s services is encrypted in transit.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 13.

2. Information the Game stores on your device

The Game saves your progress (for example cash, gates, planes, upgrade levels, milestones, daily-reward streak, boost timers, purchase flags and the time you last played) in local storage on your device. We use the last-played time to work out how much income you earned while away. This data stays on your device; we do not receive it and it is not linked to your identity. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and the Game does not use your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Game updates: on Android, the app checks idlegamesusa.github.io for game updates when you are online (it requests a small version.json file and, if a newer game version is available, downloads it). The request carries no identifier and none of your game data is sent; as with any website, GitHub Pages, which hosts these files, can see your IP address.

4. Advertising and personalization

Ads in the Android app may be personalized (based on your advertising ID and interests inferred by Google) or non-personalized (based on context such as the app and general location). You can control this on your device:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • You can also manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Opting out does not remove ads; it limits personalization. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later where the app offers a privacy-options entry, or through your device’s ad settings.

5. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game is not designed for, or submitted to, Google Play’s Designed for Families program. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please email us at idlegamesusa@gmail.com and we will help have it removed (including by contacting the relevant service provider where needed).

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data: game progress is stored on your device only. There are no accounts. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.
  • Third-party data: data held by Google (AdMob, Play Billing, Play Games) is retained according to Google’s policies. You can manage or delete Play Games data in the Play Games app and your Google Account settings.
  • Server-held data: we do not currently operate a server that stores player data. If we later hold any such data, you can ask us to delete it by emailing idlegamesusa@gmail.com; we will respond within a reasonable time (and within 30 days where the law requires).

7. Security

We keep the amount of data we handle to a minimum, and progress stays on your device. Data sent over the network by the app (update checks) and by Google’s services (such as AdMob) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International users, GDPR and CCPA

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR/UK GDPR): you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Where we rely on consent (for example, personalized ads), you can withdraw it through the consent form or your device settings. Since we do not currently hold server-side personal data about you, most requests concerning ads, billing and Play Games should be directed to Google as well.

California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states: you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Advertising partners such as Google AdMob may use identifiers for interest-based advertising; you can opt out using the device controls in section 4. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To exercise any right, email idlegamesusa@gmail.com. The Game is operated from the United States, and information handled by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries.
