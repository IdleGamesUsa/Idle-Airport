Privacy Policy

Idle Airport · by Idle Games USA · Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Idle Airport (the “Game”) stores and what information third-party services used by the Game may collect. “We”, “us” and “Idle Games USA” refer to the developer of the Game. Contact: idlegamesusa@gmail.com.

Status note (October 9, 2026): the Android app is in testing on Google Play. Optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob are active in the Android app, and Google’s User Messaging Platform shows a consent form where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). Optional in-app purchases use Google Play Billing; they are switched on only when the products are available in Google Play. From app version 1.2.0, the Android app also offers optional Google Play Games sign-in for leaderboards and cloud saves, and an optional reminder notification. From app version 1.3.0, the Game also uses a small game server that we run on Google Firebase for leaderboards, limited-time events, redeem codes and purchase checks (see “Game server” below). We do not use any analytics service of our own.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-up is needed to play. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. From version 1.3.0 the Game creates an anonymous game-server account for you automatically (a random ID, no email or password).
  • Your pilot name, your pilot’s look and your scores are shown to other players on the in-game leaderboards.
  • Your game progress is saved locally on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The Android app shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Optional purchases use Google Play Billing. Optional leaderboards and cloud saves use Google Play Games Services. These services are provided by Google and are governed by Google’s own policies as well as this one.
  • Data sent over the network by the app and by Google’s services is encrypted in transit.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 13.

2. Information the Game stores on your device

The Game saves your progress (for example cash, gates, planes, upgrade levels, milestones, daily-reward streak, boost timers, purchase flags and the time you last played) in local storage on your device. We use the last-played time to work out how much income you earned while away. This data stays on your device (and, only if you turn on cloud saves, in your Google account; see section 3); we do not receive it. The Game may ask you for a pilot name: a nickname of your choice that is shown inside the Game and stored with your progress. It is optional and does not need to be your real name. We do not ask for your email address, phone number or location, and the Game does not use your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Game updates: on Android, the app checks idlegamesusa.github.io for game updates when you are online (it requests a small version.json file and, if a newer game version is available, downloads it). The request carries no identifier and none of your game data is sent; as with any website, GitHub Pages, which hosts these files, can see your IP address.

Save backups: the Android app keeps backup copies of your save on your device (inside the app’s private storage) so it can recover your progress if the main save is damaged. A small on-device log of recent errors, ad events and purchase events is kept for the “Diagnostics” section you can copy into a bug report; it is never sent anywhere automatically.

Game settings file: the Game downloads a small settings file (remote-config.json) from idlegamesusa.github.io at start-up and every few hours (for example, the dates of limited-time cosmetic rewards, or the oldest app version that is still supported). The request carries no identifier and sends none of your game data; GitHub Pages can see your IP address, as with any website.

Reminder notification (optional): on Android you can allow one local notification that tells you when your airport’s offline earnings are full. It is scheduled on your device by the app itself when you leave the Game and cancelled when you come back; no data is sent to us or to a notification server. The Game asks first, and you can switch it off at any time in the Game’s Settings or in your device’s notification settings.

App updates: the Android app may use Google Play’s in-app update feature to tell you when a newer version is available in Google Play. This check is made by the Google Play Store on your device.

2a. Game server (Google Firebase), from version 1.3.0

From app version 1.3.0 (and the matching web version), the Game talks to a small game server that Idle Games USA runs on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Functions and Cloud Firestore, hosted by Google in the United States). It is used for the in-game leaderboards (Ranks), limited-time events and their schedule, event prizes, redeem codes and checking purchases. The Game keeps working without it (for example offline).

4. Advertising and personalization

Ads in the Android app may be personalized (based on your advertising ID and interests inferred by Google) or non-personalized (based on context such as the app and general location). You can control this on your device:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • You can also manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Opting out does not remove ads; it limits personalization. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later where the app offers a privacy-options entry, or through your device’s ad settings.

5. Children’s privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game is not designed for, or submitted to, Google Play’s Designed for Families program. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please email us at idlegamesusa@gmail.com and we will help have it removed (including by contacting the relevant service provider where needed).

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data: game progress and backups are stored on your device. There is no sign-up with us (the anonymous game-server account is described in section 2a). Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes them. The Game’s Settings also offer “Reset progress”.
  • Third-party data: data held by Google (AdMob, Play Billing, Play Games leaderboard scores and cloud saves) is retained according to Google’s policies. You can manage or delete your Play Games data, including saved games, in the Google Play Games app and your Google Account settings (for example, Play Games → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data).
  • Server-held data: leaderboard entries, redeemed codes, purchase checks and prize records on our Firebase game server (section 2a) are kept while you play and removed when no longer needed (inactive leaderboard entries after 12 months; purchase records as long as needed for refunds and accounting). You can ask us to delete your server data by emailing idlegamesusa@gmail.com with your player ID from the Game’s Settings; we will respond within a reasonable time (and within 30 days where the law requires).

7. Security

We keep the amount of data we handle to a minimum, and progress stays on your device unless you choose cloud saves. Data sent over the network by the app (update checks, the settings file, the game server) and by Google’s services (such as AdMob) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
