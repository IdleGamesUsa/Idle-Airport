Thank you for testing! Version 1.2.0 is rolling out to the test group on Google Play. Your progress carries over: just update the app and keep playing. Details below may still change before launch.

✈️Version 1.2.0 New

What's new in Idle Airport 1.2.0.

  • Prestige now lets you keep your cash. Whatever is left after paying the price comes with you.
  • Leaderboards are live: Lifetime Cash and Weekly Cash, through Google Play Games.
  • Cloud saves: sign in to Google Play Games and your airport follows you to a new phone or a reinstall.
  • New Settings screen (gear button): reminders, Play Games, cloud save, help and patch notes.
  • Optional reminder when your offline earnings are full. We only ask after the tutorial.
  • Tap your ticket count to see today's free-ticket limit.
  • New ground crew: a tug pushes planes back, a marshaller waves them off, and passengers carry luggage.
  • A short tutorial for new players. You can replay it from Settings.
  • Safer saves with automatic backups, a fix for the game going blank, and less battery use while idle.
  • Feedback: idlegamesusa@gmail.com

📝Version 1.1.0 Earlier

Notes from the previous test build (version 1.1.0 / v3). Some items below are now live in 1.2.0 (for example leaderboards).

🖼️Commemorative frames New

Two limited-time pilot frames to say thank you to our earliest players. They're free and automatic: open the game while a frame is available and it's yours to keep forever, together with a matching pilot title.

🔌 Beta Tester · Circuit BoardLegendary animated frame + the "Beta Tester" title. Earned by playing during testing, from October 5, 2026 until the public launch.
🏁 Founding Pilot · Racing StripesLegendary animated frame + the "Founding Pilot" title. Earned by playing any time from October 6, 2026 through the end of the first week after public launch.
Testing now? You'll earn both. Find them in your Pilot profile → Dresser → Frames and tap Equip, and your frame shows wherever your pilot appears. We'll announce the exact end dates closer to launch.

⚡Ad boost improvements Improved

  • Ads now load in the background and retry automatically if one isn't ready, so "Watch ad" works more often on the first tap.
  • During testing, if no ad is available after a retry, you still get your boost along with a short "enjoy your boost on us" message. Closing an ad early still means no reward.
  • Where privacy rules require it (for example in the EU and the UK), the game asks for your ad consent first, and you can change your choice any time from the Store tab.

🛒Store purchases Coming soon

Purchases through Google Play are rolling out shortly after this update: ticket packs, Lifetime 2x Pay, Offline Storage I and II (longer offline earnings, up to 12 and 24 hours) and Ad-Free Rewards (every ad reward instantly, no video). Until they switch on, the Store shows "Store coming soon".

Tickets are still free: you earn one with every plane departure you watch, plus daily rewards and milestones.

🎟️Redeem codes Coming soon

There's a new Redeem code box at the bottom of the Store tab. It isn't active yet. Later we'll use it for promo and tester codes, so keep an eye on our Discord.

🏆Leaderboards Coming soon

Online leaderboards through Google Play Games are on the way, with boards for lifetime cash, monthly cash and cargo tonnes shipped in cargo events. The Ranks tab stays hidden until they're switched on, and signing in will be optional.

🛠️Stability fixes Fixed

General stability and reliability improvements under the hood, so the game runs more smoothly on more devices.
